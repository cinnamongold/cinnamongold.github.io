Why is the YLP area separate?

There are multiple reasons this part of the website is separated from the rest of the website...

  • To maintain professionalism - Some parts of the main branch may intentionally use incorrect caps or formatting. For presentation, I want this website to look as clean as possible.
  • These projects are much larger - Since these projects are going to have a lot more features and content, I want them to have their own personal space. In the future, this YLPnet may be substituted for something else.
  • Easier navigation - Putting these directories in different sections makes this website more user-friendly and also makes it simpler to navigate as a developer.
  • Dedicated space for scripts/styles - You may have noticed this part of the website also uses different styles. It also uses separate JavaScript.
